# Startsida (/) — surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Audience: privatpersoner, företag/BRF, hyresvärdar/mäklare, mostly on phone from local search. Action: ring or send offertförfrågan. Proof: concrete per-room checklists; real facts only, no invented prices/reviews/photos. Unresolved: prices, photos, GBP link, fastighetsservice scope.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a besiktningsprotokoll: the Swedish handover form where every line is ticked, so the visitor sees exactly what is included. Refuses the category default of a photo hero over three service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Printed blankett. White ground, deep ink navy (#14202B), highlighter yellow (#F2C200) used only as marker on the one action line, cool grey rules (#C9D1D6). Ruled rows, checkbox glyphs drawn as SVG, form-field boxes, stamped "Utfört" marks. Grotesk with a point of view for headings, plain text face for body, tabular figures for phone numbers.

STORY: Visitor understands in seconds that E&I cleans in Trollhättan (and Grästorp, Lysekil, Smögen), sees what a job covers room by room, believes it because the list is concrete, and calls or sends the short offert form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Protokollhuvud across the top: "Städfirma i Trollhättan" as H1 in large ink type, form fields (Ort, Tjänst) as ruled boxes. A yellow-marked row carries the phone number 070-013 21 39 at display scale with "Ring oss" and "Be om offert" beside it. Below: first ticked rows of a flyttstäd checklist peeking in. Mobile: sticky bottom bar Ring / Offert.

FORM: Besiktningsprotokollet, Impeccable's pick, position 2 on the ordered list; seed key a06c4107. Raises kept in mind: phone number never shrinks (from The Designers Republic); service rows in fixed columns (from the split-flap board).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
